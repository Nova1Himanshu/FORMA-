2012 Toyota Camry

Source, fidelity & what is actually modeled

This viewer uses the 2012 Toyota Camry detailed finite-element model, version 5a, developed by the Center for Collision Safety and Analysis at George Mason University with Federal Highway Administration support. Researchers disassembled a real vehicle, scanned components, and catalogued their geometry. All 1,086 source part definitions are indexed here, including structural panels, door mechanisms, seat structures, trim, powertrain, steering, and suspension. Original source labels and IDs are retained.

This is a browser visualization of a crash-research model, not manufacturer service CAD and not an operating mechanical simulation. The source engine is a coarse solid representation: piston, valve, and gear internals are not present. Materials and reflections are display styling, not original textures. Larger surface meshes use 12–20 mm spatial clustering; maximum measured vertex displacement is 20.06 mm. Display coordinates are quantized to 0.1 mm. Beam and discrete elements appear as thin tubes with illustrative thickness. Null/contact surfaces and simulation connectors are hidden by default; enable Simulation helpers or isolate their branch to inspect them.

Assembly groupings are inferred from source labels and location. Exploded offsets are illustrative and do not encode a repair sequence. Source validation does not establish accuracy of this converted viewer. Download the complete source part index · Read the development report
