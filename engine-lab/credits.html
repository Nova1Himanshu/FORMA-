This is a procedurally modeled 5.7L cross-plane, pushrod V8 with two valves per cylinder. It is not Ferrari factory geometry, is not the Ferrari 458's flat-plane DOHC engine, and has not been fitted inside the Ferrari model. It is a separate educational experience.

The upstream project provides mechanical consistency checks; those do not establish manufacturer accuracy or validate real combustion, torque, temperature, emissions or vehicle performance. Its documentation notes an approximate pushrod proportion and slowed flow effects. Not every fastener or real manufacturing detail is represented.

The lab loads Three.js modules from unpkg.com and needs internet access. The Ferrari showroom remains self-contained. No purchased model has been added.
